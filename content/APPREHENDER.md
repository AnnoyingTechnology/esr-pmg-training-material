### Doubts — the reviewer's answers

18. **After compulsory delivery by the force publique** — garde à vue if legally justified, or genuine release and a later summons. Removing handcuffs or announcing freedom to leave does **not** open an immediate audition libre in that sequence. The OPJ decides; apprehension does not replace his 62-2 assessment. On the card.
19. **Uniformed APJA under art. 73** — remains force publique. « Toute personne » authorises the apprehension; it does not make an on-duty gendarme a private citizen. On the card.
20. **Guarding and escorting** — support functions consistent with art. 21 and R434-17; no OPJ/APJ-exclusive rule found. Printed « sur instruction et sous la responsabilité de l'officier » — an inference from those functions, not a special escort provision.
21. **63, 63-1, 63-3-1** — confirmed: no such powers for the APJA as such. An assistant d'enquête acts in that distinct capacity.
22. **Cass. crim. 1er octobre 1979** — supports holding the offender while awaiting police notified as promptly as circumstances permit; no fixed waiting period.

## 4. Doubts as sent to the reviewer

- **73 al. 2 read from the APJA's seat (AP8-AP9).** The exception is written for a person brought
  by a private individual. When the APJA brings the person under constraint, is it right to say the
  OPJ must then choose between GAV and release — or can he still offer an audition libre after
  releasing the constraint? The card states the text and the GAV consequence only.
- **Is an APJA arresting "as any person" under 73, or as force publique?** It matters for 73 al. 2:
  a gendarme in uniform who apprehends is, presumably, force publique. The card assumes so.
- **Surveillance and escort in GAV (AP16).** Practice is that APJA guard persons in GAV. No text
  found either way. Strike from the card if the reviewer cannot source it.
- **Audition by an APJA.** Can an APJA, trained under art. 21, hear a person in **audition libre**
  (61-1) or in GAV? Not on the card; open question shared with the APJA draft.
- **Handcuffs — case law.** Cass. crim. 8 juin 2005, n° 04-85.795 (the 803 assessment is not by
  itself a basis for prosecuting the officer) — found, not used.
