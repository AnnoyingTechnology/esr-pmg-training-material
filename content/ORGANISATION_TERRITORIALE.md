# Organisation territoriale — source de la fiche `FICHE-territoriale-A5`

**Status: built 22 September 2026.** Replaces a raster chart (`IMAGE-A5-sur-A4.pdf`) that was an
AI-generated PNG in a handwriting face. Redrawn as vector CSS in the deck's house style; the page
that placed the image (`build/image-a5.html`) and its out-of-repo symlink have been removed.

## Provenance

`[JUL]` The content is the author's, **already fact-checked on his side**. It was reproduced faithfully:
no fact was altered, added or removed during the redraw. Layout was adapted to A5 — the two side
notes of the original sit under the chain instead of beside the first two echelons.

One change, editorial only: the original read `COMMANDANT DE COMPAGINIE`. Corrected to
**COMPAGNIE**.

## What was independently checked `[REV]`

- **EDCF** — the *escadrons départementaux de contrôle des flux* were created on **1 October 2025**,
  replacing the *escadrons départementaux de sécurité routière* (91 in métropole, 4 in the DOM).
  Confirmed on gendarmerie.interieur.gouv.fr. Printed in the sources line, since it dates the chart.
