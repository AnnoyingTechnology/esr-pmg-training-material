# Chronologie du carnet PMG — ordre du recueil

**What this is.** The chapter structure of the author's own notebook from his *préparation militaire
gendarmerie* (PMG 2026): the topics in the order the sessions came, one line per notebook page. It is
published because it is only a chronology — topic titles, no content. The notebook itself stays
private (`notes_julien*`, locally excluded).

**What it is not.** It is **not the PMG syllabus** and says nothing about what is examined: it records
the order of one candidate's sessions, in one centre, in 2026. The deck uses it for **one thing only —
the order of the pages** in `out/FICHES-PMG-A5.pdf` (`build/package.sh`). Cards are still written from
the code and reviewed (`AGENTS.md` § 8); nothing on a card refers to this file.

## 2. The resulting order of the recueil

1. **Notebook chronology** (pages 1-31): G1-G7 (gendarmerie) · S1-S11 (statut & déontologie) · F1-F5
   (force & armes) · I1-I7 (l'infraction) · P1-P3 (police judiciaire) · R1-R8 (sécurité routière) ·
   E1-E18 (enquête).
2. **Not in the notebook** — the author's general legal background (*culture générale*): J1-J12.
3. **Lexique** (L) last, as the reference for every card.

Where a notebook page feeds several cards, the order follows the page (left, then right). F4 (sécurité
de l'armement) has no page of its own; it stays with the armement cards, after F3. R7-R8 extend the
route cards; E4 (the délits that open article 73) follows E3. Three cards the notebook does not cover
sit next to their closest topic: P1 (frontière police administrative / police judiciaire) opens the
police judiciaire chapter (p. 20); E8 (vie d'une affaire pénale) and E9 (action publique) come before
E10, with the enquête préliminaire and the enquêteur's role (pp. 24-25).

Since 3 October 2026 the card IDs follow this order — category letter, rank in the category — so the
IDs run in sequence through the PDF. `NUMEROTATION.md` maps the numbers used before that date.
