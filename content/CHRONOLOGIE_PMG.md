# Chronologie du carnet PMG — ordre du recueil

**What this is.** The chapter structure of the author's own notebook from his *préparation militaire
gendarmerie* (PMG 2026): the topics in the order the sessions came, one line per notebook page. It is
published because it is only a chronology — topic titles, no content. The notebook itself stays
private (`notes_julien*`, locally excluded).

**What it is not.** It is **not the PMG syllabus** and says nothing about what is examined: it records
the order of one candidate's sessions, in one centre, in 2026. The deck uses it for **one thing only —
the order of the pages** in `out/FICHES-PMG-A5.pdf` (`build/package.sh`). Cards are still written from
the code and reviewed (`AGENTS.md` § 8); nothing on a card refers to this file.

## 2. The resulting order of the recueil

1. **Notebook chronology** (pages 1-31): Frise · G1 · Organisation · G2 · Territoriale · Galons · G3 ·
   D1 · D2 · D6 · D5 · D3 · D4 · D9 · D10 · D7 · D8 · D11 · A5 · A1 · A2 · A3 · A4 · 07 · 23 · 06 ·
   24 · 25 · 22 · 22 bis · 04 · 10 · 30 · R1-R6 · 31 · 32 · 33 · 33 bis · 26 · 27 · 09 · 14 · 11 · 12 ·
   35 · 36 · 37 · 38 · 13 · 28 · 29.
2. **Not in the notebook** — the author's general legal background (*culture générale*), in their
   original order: 01 · 02 · 03 · 05 · 08 · 15 · 16 · 17 · 18 · 19 · 20 · 21.
3. **Lexique** (34) last, as the reference for every card.

Where a notebook page feeds several cards, the order follows the page (left, then right). A3 (sécurité
de l'armement) has no page of its own; it stays with the armement cards, after A2. Three cards the
notebook does not cover sit next to their closest topic: 04 (frontière police administrative / police
judiciaire) opens the police judiciaire chapter (p. 20); 09 (vie d'une affaire pénale) and 14 (action
publique) come before 11, with the enquête préliminaire and the enquêteur's role (pp. 24-25). Card
numbers are unchanged: a card keeps its number wherever it sits in the recueil.
