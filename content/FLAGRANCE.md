### Doubts — the reviewer's answers

9. **Art. 54** — amended by loi n° 2026-798, art. 55, in force 20 August 2026. Names the APJ under OPJ control, **not the APJA**.
10. **D7** — names OPJ and APJ; no basis to read APJA into it. « Concourt », grounded in art. 21, is right.
11. **Indices apparents** — keep: Cass. crim. 25 juin 2014, n° 14-81.647. « Apparent » is not only visual (corroborated information can suffice); a search lawful on another basis may reveal an incidental flagrant offence. On the card.
12. **Temps très voisin** — no universal figure; the absolute « three days » example removed.
13. **Art. 61 penalty** — none in the current text; history not conclusively verified; compulsory appearance remains available. Nothing on the card.
14. **Art. 19** — confirmed: prompt notification; transmission of the records on completion.
15. **Art. 60** — OPJ, or APJ or assistant d'enquête under his control; urgency no longer a condition. Applied.
16. **74-2** — six categories: specified arrest warrants after referral for trial; trial-court / JAP arrest warrants; enforceable final unsuspended custodial sentences ≥ 1 year (incl. specified revocations); FIJAIT breaches; FIJAIS breaches; specified enforcement decisions producing imprisonment > 1 year. The card refers to « l'un des cas limitativement prévus — notamment… ».
17. **434-4 aggravation** — covers gendarmes whose functions contribute to establishing the truth; intentional obstruction still required.

## 4. Doubts as sent to the reviewer

- **Art. 54 and the 2026 law.** The module's reference document said loi n° 2026-798 added the APJ to
  54. A search engine shows a version of 54 naming the APJ "sous le contrôle", but not which law or
  date. **Confirm: text, date, and that the APJA is not included.**
- **D7 — APJA or not.** D7 names « officiers et agents de police judiciaire ». Does the regulatory
  part use "agents de police judiciaire" in the strict art. 20 sense? If yes, the APJA preserves the
  scene only as seconder; if the courts read it broadly, he is directly bound. The card hedges with
  « concourt ».
- **« Indices apparents »** (FL10) — standard teaching, no decision found. Cite one or strike it.
- **« Temps très voisin »** (FL9) — is there a usable benchmark from case law (hours)? The card
  deliberately gives none.
- **Art. 61 penalty.** Older versions punished leaving the scene despite the OPJ's prohibition; the
  current text shows none. Confirm it was repealed, not moved.
- **74-2 categories.** Six categories reported; not read in full. The card says only « mandat
  d'arrêt, ou condamnée à une peine privative de liberté, dans les cas de l'article ».
