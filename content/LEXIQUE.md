### Doubts — the reviewer's answers

23. **Chose jugée** — replaced by the reviewer's wording: autorité = effect attached to what was decided, without removing appeals; force = no suspensive remedy left; irrévocable = remedies exhausted, cassation included, subject to exceptional revision.
24. **Composition pénale** — judicial validation remains the default, limited exemption since 2019. Applied as « validée par un juge, sauf exception légale ».
25. **Commissaire de justice** — the current title; some CPP provisions still say huissier. The substantive fix is that signification **is** a notification.
26. **Force probante** — 430 simple renseignement · 431 specified délits, proof to the contrary · 433 inscription de faux under special laws · 537 contraventions. Applied.
27. **Jours-amende** — imprisonment for the **full** number of unpaid days. Applied.
28. **Mise en accusation** also for the cour criminelle départementale (181-1); the information is still compulsory for crimes (79). Applied.
29. **Convocation 390-1** — not in the APJA capacity alone; an assistant d'enquête may, in that capacity. Carrying a document ≠ notifying it. Applied as « pas par l'agent adjoint ».
30. **Texte consolidé** — confirmed: consolidation is informative; the JO publications are authoritative.
31. **NATINF** — public DACG nomenclature; identifies, does not create, the offence. Applied.
32. **Missing terms** — optional: prescription, contradictoire, suspect / prévenu / accusé, réquisition. **Not added**; candidates for a later revision.
