### Doubts — the reviewer's answers

1. **Oath — yes.** Gendarmerie reservist APJA take an oath in a public hearing before the president of the tribunal judiciaire (décret n° 2013-874 du 27 septembre 2013, art. 1, 2, 4). **Now on the card**, one line.
2. **Designation** — art. 21 gives the status by law; no OPJ-style individual habilitation identified. Oath, assignment, training and competence for a given act remain distinct. Not on the card.
3. **Joint training arrêté** — not identified by the reviewer. Ordinary PMG/APJA training **cannot be presumed** equivalent. The card states the condition and adds a piège: « la formation fixée par arrêté est une condition — elle ne se présume pas ».
4. **Hearing a suspect** — art. 21 does not exclude a suspect; with 61-1, an audition libre is supported subject to training, OPJ control, the exclusions and the suspect's safeguards. Implementation unverified. « Never in garde à vue » is **not established** by 63/63-1, which govern other acts. **Nothing printed** on either point.
5. **New 20-1 route** — exists in law; implementing decree not verified. Absence not proven. Card keeps the legislative statement.
6. **Retirees** — confirmed: at least five years as OPJ/APJ; refresher training after a break of more than one year (R15-17-1). On the card.
7. **Art. 21 wording** — « en se conformant aux ordres de leurs chefs » confirmed.
8. **1° quater** — also covers Paris administration controllers (voie publique). Label adjusted.

## 4. Doubts as sent to the reviewer

- **Serment.** The module outline lists « serment et affectation ». I found **no oath** required of a
  gendarmerie APJA (1° bis) in the CPP. Police municipale APJA are sworn (CSI L511-2) — not our case.
  **Is there one** (code de la défense, réserve regulations, a CPP R/D article)? Not on the card.
- **Affectation / habilitation.** Does an APJA 1° bis need any individual designation to exercise
  art. 21 missions, beyond being a serving reservist? Not on the card.
- **Training for declarations.** The *arrêté conjoint* setting the training obligation — exists? date?
  Does the reservist APJA training already count? Not on the card beyond « formation préalable ».
- **The new 20-1 route.** Implementing decree not found. Is it in force in practice? The card says
  only what the law says.
- **R15-17-1 / décret 2022-1113** reportedly requires **five years** as OPJ/APJ for retirees to regain
  APJ status in the reserve. `[SEC]` — not on the card; worth a line if confirmed.
- **Can an APJA question a suspect?** Art. 21 speaks of declarations from "toutes les personnes
  susceptibles de fournir des indices…" — does that include a person suspected (audition libre) or a
  person in garde à vue? My reading: not in garde à vue; unclear for audition libre. Not on the card.
