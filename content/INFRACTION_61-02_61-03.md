# Éléments et classification de l'infraction — source des fiches 23, 24, 25

**Status: built 22 September 2026, fact-checked and substantially corrected.**

## Provenance — read this before editing

The *coverage target* came from two gendarmerie fiches de documentation, **F61-02 (l'infraction)**
and **F61-03 (la classification des infractions)**. Those documents are **© CPMGN** and carry an
explicit footer: « L'usage, l'impression, la copie, la publication ou la diffusion sont strictement
interdits en dehors de la Gendarmerie nationale. » They are gitignored (`61-*.txt`) and must never be
committed.

They were used **only to establish what the deck was missing**. Every card here is written from the
code articles, with its own structure, wording and examples — see `AGENTS.md` § 8 bis.

## Why that distinction turned out to matter for accuracy, not just for licensing

The fact-check was brutal on the first draft, and almost every finding traced back to my having
reproduced a *pedagogical simplification* rather than the statute:

| Drafted from the simplification | What the code actually says |
|---|---|
| Responsabilité pénale « répare le trouble à l'ordre social » | It **sanctions an offence**. Damage is required only where the offence's own definition requires it |
| Intention = willing the act **and its result** | Intention bears on the offence **as the text defines it**, not on every consequence — violences volontaires ayant entraîné la mort sans intention de la donner remain intentional |
| Mise en danger délibérée = willing to breach a safety obligation | Art. 223-1 needs a **manifestly deliberate** breach of a **particular** obligation, **directly** exposing another to an **immediate** risk of death or serious injury |
| Contraventions: intent is always irrelevant | **In principle** only. Some require voluntary conduct (R625-1 violences volontaires); force majeure excludes the contravention |
| Flagrance available for all délits | Only for délits **punishable by imprisonment** (CPP 53, 67) |
| Complicité for contraventions: provocation/instructions only | That is the general rule (R610-2 → 121-7 al. 2), but **special texts also punish aide ou assistance**, e.g. R625-1 |
| Concours: « non-cumul des peines » | One penalty **of each kind**, within the highest applicable maximum; separate proceedings execute cumulatively with possible confusion (132-3, 132-4, 132-7) |
| Sursis probatoire: ≤ 5 years | ≤ 5 years, **≤ 10 in légal recidivism**; concerns *emprisonnement*, not *réclusion* (132-41, 132-42) |
| Récidive contraventionnelle: only if the règlement provides | Also: same contravention **within one year**; and the law may make repetition a **délit**, with a three-year window (132-11) |
| Délaissement (223-3) illustrates commission par omission | 223-3 requires a **positive act** of definitive abandonment. It cannot illustrate a general equation of omission with commission |
| Instantanée/continue split governs law, jurisdiction and prescription alike | Solid for **prescription**. Jurisdiction is governed by CPP 382, which also recognises residence, arrest and detention; applicable law must respect 112-1 and rétroactivité *in mitius* |

**The lesson for future cards:** a teaching document's summary of the law is not the law. Use it to
find the topic, then read the article.

## Still open `[???]`

- **Cour criminelle départementale thresholds.** It tries crimes punishable by 15 or 20 years plus
  connected délits; **legal recidivism ceased to be an exclusion on 25 July 2026**. The reviewer
  warned that "assises whenever more than 20 years is incurred" is unsafe where recidivism raises
  the penalty. The card states the allocation without that edge case.
- **"There is no legal definition of infraction in French law."** Reviewer would not certify the
  negative claim about all French law. The card now gives the working definition without asserting
  the absence.
- **Exercice illégal de la médecine** as the example of an infraction d'habitude — CSP L4161-1 also
  covers a single listed act, so the card marks it *en partie seulement*.
