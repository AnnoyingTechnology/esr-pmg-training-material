# Amende forfaitaire — source de la fiche 22

**Status: built 22 September 2026, fact-checked.** Sourced entirely to Légifrance — no restricted

## Why the card exists

Fiche 06 carries a piège box reading « Maximum légal ≠ amende forfaitaire. Forfaitaire, minorée et
majorée obéissent à leurs propres montants » — and then never gives those amounts anywhere in the
deck. Fiche 22 closes that. AFD appeared only in passing on R3 and R6.

## Fact-check `[REV]` — gpt-6-astra, 22 September 2026

Every figure verified against the consolidated text in force on 22 September 2026. All claims came
back CORRECT. Four refinements were applied:

1. **Personne morale ×5 is article 495-24-1**, not 495-17.
2. **The victim's right is article 495-24-2** — and the tribunal, sitting with a single judge, rules
   on the *civil interests only*; the extinguished action publique does not revive.
3. **The three-year limit is measured on the maximum encouru**, not on the sentence sought or passed.
4. **Abandon illégal de déchets was removed from the AFD table.** L541-46 VIII still prints
   1 000 / 1 500 / 2 500 €, but the offence now carries **four years**' imprisonment — above the
   ceiling set by Conseil constitutionnel decision 2019-778 DC, § 252. The reviewer would not certify
   the procedure as currently usable. The conflict is now taught on the card as a piège instead,
   which is more useful than the row would have been.

## Currency warning

**Usage de stupéfiants moved from 200 € to 500 € on 20 August 2026** — loi n° 2026-798 du 18 août
2026, article 25 I, 1°, amending code de la santé publique L3421-1 (minorée 150 → 400, majorée
450 → 1 000). Any revision material printed before that date is wrong on this line. The card carries
the date for exactly this reason.

**Article 495-17 is repealed by ordonnance n° 2025-1091 du 19 novembre 2025, effective 1 January
2029.** In force on 22 September 2026. Same CPP rewrite already flagged in AGENTS.md § 10.
